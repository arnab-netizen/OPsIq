> Something I see a lot: a business is profitable on paper but can't comfortably
> cover payroll and rent. The usual cause is receivables timing, not losses.
> Quick check: compare cash on hand + what's realistically collectible soon
> against what's due in the next ~30 days. If profit looks fine but that
> comparison is tight, the gap is usually slow collections, a debt payment, or
> a one-off purchase — worth naming specifically.
