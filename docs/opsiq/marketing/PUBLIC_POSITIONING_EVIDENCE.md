## Population caveat (mandatory wherever S1/S2/S3 percentages appear)

Respondents are 767 US operations and supply-chain leaders surveyed by PwC.
Do not describe the percentages as representative of all businesses, SMEs, owner-led companies, OpsIQ users,
or global businesses. Do not say PwC "predicts", "recommends", "validates" or "proves" OpsIQ, or that
"businesses need OpsIQ".
