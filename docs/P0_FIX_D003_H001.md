## Findings Fixed

### D-003: Intake confirm — no routing after confirmation

**File:** `src/app/(authenticated)/owner/intake/page.tsx`

**Before:** `confirm()` called the API, refreshed intake list, and left the owner on the same page with no indication that diagnosis was ready or where to go next.

**After:** On successful confirm, a success banner appears:
> "Data confirmed. OpsIQ is now ready to analyze your business. [Go to Command Center →]"

The banner links to `/owner`. Owner has a clear path forward.

**Changes:**
- Added `useRouter` import
- Added `confirmed` state variable
- `confirm()` sets `confirmed = true` on success
- Success banner renders above the business list when `confirmed === true`

---

### H-001: No-data message — wrong link, wrong context

**File:** `src/app/(authenticated)/owner/page.tsx`

**Before (lines 113–115):**
```tsx
No business condition yet. Run a diagnosis in{" "}
<Link href="/owner/finance" className="underline">Finance</Link> to populate the command center.
```

This told owners to run a Finance diagnosis before they had entered any data. The link went to `/owner/finance`, not to the intake form.

**After:**
```tsx
Your OpsIQ diagnosis requires business data.{" "}
<Link href="/owner/intake" className="underline">Upload your data →</Link>
```

Owner arriving with no data sees a direct path to the intake form.
