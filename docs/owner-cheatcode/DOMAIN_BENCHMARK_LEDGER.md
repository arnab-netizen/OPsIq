# Domain Benchmark Ledger — Owner Cheat-Code

Honest benchmark of each domain for the **owner's actual operating outcome** — not
by feature count. OpsIQ is a decision/execution/safety layer, not a point-tool
replacement; it does not aim to match every narrow feature of a dedicated app.

**Benchmark basis (this session):**
- Repo-local: `docs/BEST_IN_CLASS_CREDIBILITY_RELIABILITY_AUDIT.md`, `docs/BUYER_PROOF_PACKET.md`, `docs/ADVERSARIAL_BENCHMARK_EXPANSION.md`, `src/domain/benchmark/*`, `src/domain/trust-benchmarks/*`.
- Named commercial-app capabilities below are **PROVISIONAL** (general product knowledge, **not** web-verified this session). Per the no-fake-benchmarking rule, a verified feature-by-feature benchmark is registered as **GAP-010 BENCHMARK_RESEARCH_REQUIRED**.

**Owner-outcome comparison dimensions (§6):** accuracy, practical usefulness, owner
workload reduction, time-to-decision, time-to-execution, financial safety,
proof/auditability, staff accountability, outcome measurement, cross-domain
awareness, risk prevention, local context, Work-Package generation, unsafe-decision
blocking, outcome learning.

**Classification legend:** BELOW / EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE / BETTER_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE / PARTIAL_CONTINUE_REQUIRED.

## 1. Finance / Budget / Cash Control
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Xero, Tally.
- **Their strength:** deep bookkeeping, invoicing, tax filing, reconciliation.
- **Their weakness (for the owner's decision outcome):** record-keeping, not decision-safety; they do not block an unsafe discount/hire/spend or connect cash to strategy.
- **OpsIQ matched:** cash runway, margin, break-even, budget/spend tracking (`owner-budget`, `owner-finance`).
- **OpsIQ exceeds (connected loop):** below-floor discount BLOCKED (`margin-safety-gate`), cash-critical spend/hire BLOCKED (`cash-safety-gate`), capital allocation defers offensive spend in EMERGENCY (`capital-allocation`), spend governance (SOD/split-spend), all feeding Next Best Move + Work Package + proof + outcome.
- **Intentionally rejected:** full general-ledger bookkeeping (owner burden; use an accounting tool for records).
- **Scenario:** RW-W02/04/06/07/10 + domain-hardening FINANCE — all pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (BETTER on decision-safety; feature-parity for records intentionally not pursued). Verified benchmark: GAP-010.

## 2. Sales / Customers / Revenue
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce.
- **Their strength:** contact/pipeline management, email sequences, dashboards.
- **Their weakness:** advice/tracking, not prepared executable work tied to cash safety.
- **OpsIQ matched:** pipeline stages, follow-up, retention/reactivation, reviews (`owner-sales`, work-package artifacts).
- **OpsIQ exceeds:** generates the actual customer script + call list + tracker (Work Package), defines proof, measures outcome, ranks vs opportunity cost.
- **Scenario:** RW-W01/W03/W08 + domain-hardening SALES — pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (BETTER on prepared-work + proof). GAP-010 for verified parity.

## 3. Marketing / Growth
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** Mailchimp, Meta/Google Ads Manager.
- **Their strength:** campaign execution, targeting, ad delivery, analytics.
- **Their weakness:** happily spend even when ROI is negative / cash is unsafe.
- **OpsIQ matched:** campaign plan, ROI tracking (`owner-marketing` `campaignRoiPct`, wasted-spend finding).
- **OpsIQ exceeds:** vanity/low-ROI spend downgraded, GROWTH spend BLOCKED under cash risk, campaign becomes a Work Package with a stop rule + proof.
- **Scenario:** RW-W01/W06 + domain-hardening MARKETING — pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (BETTER on spend-safety). GAP-010.

## 4. Operations / Delivery / Quality
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** Trello, Asana, monday.com, SOP tools.
- **Their strength:** task boards, checklists, workflow tracking.
- **Their weakness:** track work but do not gate scaling or tie ops to cash/quality outcome.
- **OpsIQ matched:** SOPs, checklists, daily task board (Work Package artifacts).
- **OpsIQ exceeds:** scale-readiness gate blocks scaling unstable ops (`scale-readiness`), ops action carries proof + outcome, cross-domain (ops stress blocks sales push).
- **Scenario:** domain-hardening OPERATIONS + sim-16 sales-vs-ops — pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (BETTER on scale-safety). GAP-010.

## 5. Workforce / Training / Accountability
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** BambooHR, Deputy, When I Work.
- **Their strength:** HR records, scheduling, attendance.
- **Their weakness:** do not detect fake completion or enforce proof of work quality.
- **OpsIQ matched:** staff scorecards, training plans, task assignment (`owner-mode`, work-package).
- **OpsIQ exceeds:** fake-completion detection + proof validation (`verification-engine`, `proof.service`), owner-workload transfer measurement.
- **Scenario:** domain-hardening WORKFORCE + sim-4/12 fake completion — pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (BETTER on anti-fake-work). GAP-010.

## 6. Compliance / Risk / Governance
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** ClearTax, legal/compliance SaaS, license trackers.
- **Their strength:** jurisdiction-specific filings, verified legal content.
- **Their weakness:** OpsIQ must NOT claim verified legal content (hallucination risk).
- **OpsIQ matched:** compliance checklists, license/expiry tracking (`owner-mode/compliance-boundary`).
- **OpsIQ exceeds (safety):** compliance gate fails closed on unverified jurisdiction/expert source, blocks unsourced advice from high-risk decisions (`compliance-gate`, `business-wisdom`).
- **Honest limit:** OpsIQ is a confidence-gate + escalation layer, **not** a source of verified legal answers — it escalates to professional review. This is intentional, not a weakness.
- **Scenario:** domain-hardening COMPLIANCE + sim-9 — pass.
- **Classification:** **EQUAL_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** for owner risk-gating (defers verified legal content to professionals by design). GAP-010 for jurisdiction-content benchmarking.

## 7. Strategy / Expansion / Scaling
- **Dedicated tools (provisional):** LivePlan, business-plan/OKR tools.
- **Their strength:** plan templates, forecasts, OKR tracking.
- **Their weakness:** optimistic planning; no honest stop/pivot/sell/exit; no risk-adjusted wealth ranking.
- **OpsIQ matched:** scenario planning, expansion readiness (`owner-strategy`).
- **OpsIQ exceeds:** wealth-path classifier (trap/dead-end/owner-job), risk-adjusted wealth + opportunity cost, stop/pivot/sell/exit honesty, scale-readiness gate.
- **Scenario:** RW-W02/04/05/09 + domain-hardening STRATEGY — pass.
- **Classification:** **BETTER_FOR_OWNER_USE_CASE** (uncomfortable-verdict honesty + risk-adjusted ranking not offered by planning tools). GAP-010 for verified parity.
