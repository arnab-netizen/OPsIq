# OpsIQ — Current Positioning Statement

**Date:** 2026-07-07 · Truth-control audit (PASS 34).

The single, truthful positioning of OpsIQ as it exists **today**. Use this
verbatim (or scoped down from it) anywhere OpsIQ is described. Do not add capability
beyond what this says.

## One sentence
> OpsIQ is a **governed, deterministic reasoning engine for small-business
> intervention and recovery** — proven in backend code, 911 automated tests, and
> 28 DB-backed CI simulations — that turns messy real-world business signals into
> prioritised, explained, evidence-gated, owner-approved actions, and refuses to
> fake money, autonomy, or outcomes.

## One paragraph
OpsIQ models four dimensions at once — the consulting lifecycle stage, the
business condition, the intervention mode/phase, and the human execution reality
— and drives a business from crisis toward stabilization through milestones that
**cannot be skipped and cannot complete without evidence**. It is **governed by
design**: nothing material happens without owner approval, it takes no autonomous
external action, it fabricates no financial figures, and it stays honest when a
business is unrecoverable. Today OpsIQ is a **backend + CI-proven engine**, not
yet an owner-facing product, and it has **not** been run on a real business.

## What OpsIQ is NOT (say this if asked)
- Not an autonomous agent. Not "AI that runs your business."
- Not a live product with real owners or real financial results.
- Not integrated with external systems.
- Not a guarantee of survival, recovery, growth, or profit.

## Maturity, stated plainly
- **Engine & governance:** strong — deterministic, tested, CI-gated, audited.
- **Owner-facing product (UI):** not yet built for these capabilities.
- **Real-world validation:** none yet — all proof is synthetic/controlled.
