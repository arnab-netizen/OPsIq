# Crisis Privacy & Safety Notes — PASS 32

The survival/recovery navigator uses **controlled internal-pressure fixtures** (not public text, not live data) and routes every action through the existing governed substrate.

1. **No live data / connectors / autonomous action.** Crisis inputs are controlled pressure states; nothing is fetched or executed.
2. **No fabricated financials.** No currency/percentage/ROI/runway is ever emitted; a Zod refinement rejects money in any survival summary/action/milestone. Where cash matters, the plan says "measure the real figure — none is assumed" and creates a data task.
3. **No fake optimism.** The plan never guarantees success. When cash runway, feasible revenue, owner capital, and capacity are all absent, it returns `UNRECOVERABLE_UNDER_CURRENT_CONSTRAINTS` with a restructure / controlled-shutdown review + urgent owner/expert decision — not false hope.
4. **Survival-first ordering.** Cash/continuity, customer/reputation, quality, legal/contract/tender, and capacity always outrank growth/marketing/expansion/tender-submit/discount. Growth/scale is listed as blocked-before-stabilization in every crisis (Zod-enforced).
5. **Owner-gating held.** Legal/contract/tender/spend/discount decisions are owner-approval tasks; a non-owner cannot approve them (governed bridge).
6. **Evidence + reassessment gates.** The top action and recovery milestones carry evidence requirements and reassessment steps; completion is evidence-gated by the bridge.
7. **Staff-language safety.** Staff/capacity issues route to training/SOP correction (process coaching) — never blame, discipline, firing, fraud, or negligence language.
8. **No unsafe external action.** Tender auto-submit, auto customer/tenant/buyer contact, auto spend/discount/pricing, and reckless scale are always blocked.
9. **No new substrate / no execution authority.** Every action maps to the existing ProcessExecutionTask / approval / evidence / reassessment systems; the plan proposes, the governed bridge routes and gates.
10. **Fail-closed.** An incoherent plan (unrecoverable without a terminal status/options, growth-not-blocked, owner route not owner-gated, fabricated money) is rejected by the schema and never reaches execution.

**Clean control:** a business with no pressures, no temptation, and no missing data yields a **null** plan — nothing fabricated.
