## Long description
Most owners and operators don't lack advice — they lack a clear, honest read on *where the pressure
actually is* and *what to do next*. OpsIQ is a governed business-intervention system that turns a few
inputs about your business into a structured diagnosis: a severity read, the recommended intervention
phase, the specific findings driving it, and a prioritized action plan where every action has an owner
role, a due window, and the metric it is meant to move.

It is deliberately **algorithmic, not a chatbot.** The diagnosis is deterministic and rule-based, so
the same inputs produce the same governed output — no hallucinated strategy, no paid LLM key to buy.
The customer journey (sign up → diagnose → see findings, recommendations, and actions on your
dashboard) works end-to-end with no payment provider configured. We're launching as a **free beta** to
learn from real operators before adding anything else.

OpsIQ models four dimensions at once — consulting lifecycle stage, business condition, intervention
mode/phase, and the human execution reality (owner bottlenecking, follow-through risk, accountability)
— so the recommendation is grounded in how the business actually runs, not a generic template.

## Free beta explanation
OpsIQ is free during beta. There is **no payment provider configured** — signup, diagnosis, and the
dashboard all work with no credit card and no billing of any kind. We're prioritizing real operator
feedback over monetization right now; if/when paid plans arrive, beta users will get clear notice.

## Privacy / trust explanation
- You sign up into a **private workspace**; your business inputs stay in your workspace.
- The diagnosis runs **algorithmically** — there is **no third-party AI provider** receiving your data
  to produce results.
- Authentication is a standard database-backed session cookie; we don't ask for payment details.
- Questions about data handling: **support@opsiq.com**.

## What it does today
- Public landing page explaining the product to logged-out visitors.
- Email/password signup creating a private workspace (no payment, no email-verification gate).
- A structured, deterministic **business diagnosis**: severity, intervention phase, findings,
  recommendations, and an action plan with owners, due windows, and success metrics.
- Dashboard showing the engagement, findings, recommendations, and actions.
- Works fully with **no payment provider and no paid AI key**.

## What it does not yet do
- No payments / paid plans (Stripe is dormant/optional; no Lemon Squeezy).
- No automated email verification or self-serve password reset (recovery via support@opsiq.com).
- No third-party integrations or multi-user team collaboration yet.
- No mobile app (responsive web only).
