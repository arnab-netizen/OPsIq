CRITICAL INSIGHT: Dimension-Based vs Content-Based Pattern Matching

CURRENT ARCHITECTURE (BROKEN):
  Pattern fires if: dimensions.includes("financial_health") && dimensions.includes("operational")
  ↓
  Pattern fires regardless of actual evidence content
  ↓
  Example: BLND-006
    - Has quality_delivery + customer_retention dimensions
    - Pattern 3 fires → TRUST_QUALITY_CRISIS
    - But evidence states: "operations stable", "NPS 48 (no decline)", "repeat 72% (high)"
    - Correct diagnosis: DEMAND_FORECASTING_MISMATCH (market saturation, not quality crisis)

REQUIRED ARCHITECTURE (TO FIX):
  Pattern fires if: hasEvidence("quality defects") && hasEvidence("churn linked to quality")
  ↓
  Pattern validates content before generating diagnosis
  ↓
  Example: BLND-006
    - Evidence: "operations stable", "NPS stable but churn rising from competition"
    - Pattern validation: No quality defects → Quality Crisis pattern does NOT fire
    - Correct diagnosis: DEMAND_FORECASTING_MISMATCH (pattern 6 fires and wins)
