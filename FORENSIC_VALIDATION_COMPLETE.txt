   - Component fixes: 250 LOC, 2-3 weeks, MEDIUM risk
   - vs Full rewrite: 1200 LOC, 4-6 weeks, HIGH risk
   - vs Minimal patches: 150 LOC, 1-2 weeks, but only 13/21 accuracy

4. IDENTIFIED FAILURE MECHANISMS:
   a) Contradiction non-suppression: Patterns created despite contradictory evidence
      Example: TRUST_QUALITY_CRISIS when satisfaction metrics intact (NPS 48, repeat 72%)
   
   b) Missing unavailable-data checks: Confident diagnosis when critical factors unknown
      Example: Diagnosis despite "unavailableData" explicitly marked offer structure unfinalized
   
   c) Symptom-root-cause confusion: Symptoms (churn, margin decline) diagnosed as root causes
      Example: CUSTOMER_RETENTION_EROSION predicted when unit-cost deterioration is root
   
   d) Pattern-mapping errors: Correct patterns created but mapped to wrong diagnoses
      Example: GTM pattern created but mapped as CUSTOMER_RETENTION_EROSION
   
   e) Aggregation masking: Blended metrics accepted without disaggregation
      Example: Blended 10-month payback accepted despite cohort deterioration (7→18 months)
